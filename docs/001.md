# RevRescue Platform Overview

**RevRescue** is an AI-powered revenue retention and recovery platform built specifically for SaaS companies. It bridges the gap between passive email billing reminders and aggressive collection agencies by deploying a highly conversational, empathetic AI Voice Agent to call customers immediately after a billing event occurs.

Below is a comprehensive breakdown of everything the platform does, from its infrastructure to its business value.

---

## 1. Core Objective & Value Proposition
SaaS businesses lose significant Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) to passive churn (failed credit cards) and active churn (users clicking "cancel"). Traditional recovery methods rely on automated email drips, which are easily ignored. 

RevRescue solves this by replacing emails with a human-like phone call from an AI. The AI acts as a polite Customer Success Associate or Product Executive, engaging the customer directly to resolve the issue, resulting in vastly higher recovery rates.

---

## 2. The Trigger: Stripe Integration
RevRescue operates completely autonomously in the background. It connects directly to a SaaS company's Stripe account via webhooks. It listens 24/7 for specific billing lifecycle events:

- **`invoice.payment_failed`**: Triggered when a recurring subscription payment fails (e.g., expired card, insufficient funds).
- **`customer.subscription.deleted`**: Triggered when a user actively cancels their subscription.

When these webhooks hit the RevRescue backend, the system parses the customer data (Name, Phone Number, Email, MRR value, and Plan Tier) and queues them for recovery.

---

## 3. The Brain: Dynamic AI Playbooks & Prompting
Before picking up the phone, RevRescue determines the context of the call and selects a specific "Playbook". A playbook is a set of instructions and boundaries for the AI.

### Scenario A: Payment Failed
The AI adopts the persona of a helpful support agent. It checks if the customer has a saved card on file.
- **If Yes:** It informs the customer of the failure and asks for permission to retry the card ending in `XXXX`. If the user agrees, the system attempts the charge.
- **If No:** It informs the customer they need to update their payment method and offers to immediately email them a secure, 1-click checkout link.
- *Edge Case Handling:* If the customer is rude or profane, the system is dynamically authorized to drop the overly polite persona and match their tone.

### Scenario B: Subscription Canceled
The AI adopts the persona of a Product Executive. The goal is *not* to aggressively sell, but to listen and learn.
- It asks the user exactly where the product fell short.
- **If the issue is budget:** The AI is pre-authorized to negotiate, offering a 3-month account pause or a 50% discount to keep them active.
- **If the issue is a missing feature:** The AI thanks them, logs the feature request, and offers to add them to a VIP beta list.

---

## 4. The Voice: CALL-E Execution
Once the playbook is built, the backend pushes the job to the **CALL-E Service**. This is the voice synthesis and telephony layer that dials the customer's real phone number.
- The AI speaks with ultra-low latency, handling interruptions, pauses, and natural human conversational pacing.
- The entire conversation is recorded, transcribed, and analyzed in real-time.

---

## 5. The Nervous System: Real-Time WebSockets
While the AI is on the phone, the RevRescue backend acts as the central nervous system, streaming data to the frontend via WebSockets (`socket.io`).
- `call:started`: Emitted the moment the phone rings.
- `call:status`: Streams live updates (e.g., "in_progress", "customer_answered").
- `call:completed`: Fired when the call hangs up, carrying the final outcome.
- `metrics:updated`: Pushes live financial data (e.g., +$500 MRR Recovered) to all connected dashboards instantly.

---

## 6. The User Interface: Dashboard & Analytics
SaaS owners interact with RevRescue through a sleek, dark-mode Next.js dashboard. The UI is designed to give executives a bird's-eye view of their churn defense.

- **Overview Metrics**: Displays Total Calls, Recovered Revenue, Active Calls, and overall Conversion Rates.
- **Live Call Activity**: A real-time feed that pulses and updates as the AI makes calls in the background.
- **Call Logs**: A detailed table where owners can view the history of every intercepted webhook, read the AI's conversation transcript, and see the exact outcome (Recovered vs Failed).
- **A/B Testing Hub**: Owners can run different playbooks simultaneously to see which AI personality or discount offer yields the highest ROI.

---

## 7. The Mocking System (Developer Environment)
Because testing real credit card failures in Stripe is difficult during development, the platform includes a fully featured **Mock Payment System**. 
- A developer dashboard allows you to manually input fake customer data and simulate a webhook.
- It intercepts the mock webhook, triggers a real phone call to your test device, and dynamically prints a mock checkout link in the terminal.
- Clicking the link opens a custom-built Stripe-like checkout page to simulate a customer entering new card details, seamlessly completing the loop and updating the dashboard metrics.

---

## Summary
In short, RevRescue is an automated, empathetic, and highly analytical AI employee that works 24/7 to save canceled subscriptions and fix failed payments, turning passive churn into active revenue recovery.
